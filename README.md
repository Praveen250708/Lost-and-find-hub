# Campus Connect Hub

Smart Campus Lost & Found Hub (Top Recommendation)

The Problem: WhatsApp groups and college notice boards are flooded with messages about lost ID cards, keys, and lab coats. They get buried instantly.
The Solution: A centralized, searchable web portal specifically for your college where students can post lost or found items. [1, 2, 3, 4]

Key Features:

Dashboard: Grid view of items categorized by "Lost" (Red tag) or "Found" (Green tag).

Report Form: Simple page to upload item details (Item name, where it was lost/found, date, and contact details).

Search & Filter: Filter items by category (Electronics, Documents, Keys, Clothing) or search by keywords.

Claim Button: A button that instantly opens a WhatsApp chat or mail to the person who found the item
